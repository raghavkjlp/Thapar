About the Society

About CS-CATALYST

The official departmental society driving academic excellence, technical growth, and student leadership in the CSE Department of Thapar Institute of Engineering & Technology (TIET), Patiala.

Our Mission

To foster a dynamic academic ecosystem within the CSE Department by organizing high-impact Faculty Development Programs (FDPs), Short-Term Courses (STCs), expert talks, workshops, and student engagement initiatives that bridge the gap between academia and industry.

Our Vision

To be the most impactful departmental society at TIET — empowering students, faculty, and researchers to innovate, collaborate, and lead in an ever-evolving technological landscape, from quantum computing to artificial intelligence.

Member Responsibilities

Responsibilities of CS-CATALYST Members

Every member of the CS-CATALYST Organizing Panel plays a vital role in delivering world-class departmental events.

01

Planning & Coordination

Assist in planning, organizing, and coordinating academic, technical, and professional events of the Computer Science Department.

02

FDPs, STCs & Expert Talks

Coordinate FDPs, Short-Term Courses (STCs), expert talks, seminars, workshops, technical sessions, Open Day, and other departmental events.

03

Faculty Support

Support faculty coordinators in event planning, scheduling, logistics, venue arrangements, and resource requirements.

04

Stakeholder Coordination

Coordinate with speakers, experts, faculty members, students, and other stakeholders before and during events.

05

Participant Management

Handle student registrations, attendance, communication, and participant coordination for all events.

06

Promotions & Media

Prepare and circulate event announcements, invitations, posters, schedules, and other promotional content through digital channels.

07

Event Day Operations

Manage event-day activities including registration desks, seating, technical arrangements, hospitality, and overall coordination.

08

Documentation & Reports

Maintain attendance records, photographs, feedback forms, certificates, and assist in preparing post-event reports for future improvement.

09

Leadership & Professionalism

Demonstrate leadership, teamwork, professionalism, creativity, and problem-solving skills while handling events and representing CS-CATALYST.

10

Innovation & Ideas

Take initiative in identifying and proposing new ideas for academic, technical, and student-oriented activities that raise the department's profile.
