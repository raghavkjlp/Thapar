Recruitment Drive 2026

Join the CS-CATALYST Panel

Applications are open for the CS-CATALYST Society Organizing Panel Team for students of the Computer Science & Engineering Department, TIET. Shortlisted candidates will be invited for an interaction with esteemed faculty professors.

Who Can Apply?

Students currently enrolled in the CSE Department (B.Tech, M.Tech, Ph.D.) at TIET, Patiala who are passionate about event organization.

Selection Process

Resume shortlisting by faculty followed by a personal interaction session with esteemed professors of the CSE Department.

What You Gain

Real event management experience, faculty mentorship, certificate of recognition, leadership skills, and a strong professional network.

Application Deadline

Applications close on September 20, 2026. Shortlisted candidates will be notified within 7 working days after the deadline.

Key Responsibilities of CS-CATALYST Members (Click to expand)

  • Assist in planning, organizing, and coordinating academic, technical, and professional events.
  • Coordinate FDPs, STCs, expert talks, seminars, workshops, and departmental events.
  • Support faculty coordinators in event planning, scheduling, logistics, and resource requirements.
  • Coordinate with speakers, experts, faculty, and students before and during events.
  • Handle student registrations, attendance, communication, and participant coordination.
  • Prepare and circulate event announcements, invitations, posters, and schedules.
  • Manage event-day activities: registration desks, seating, technical arrangements, hospitality.
  • Take initiative in identifying and proposing new ideas for student-oriented activities.
  • Maintain proper documentation: attendance records, photos, feedback, reports, certificates.
  • Assist in preparing post-event reports and collecting feedback for future improvement.
  • Work as a team and take responsibility for assigned tasks ensuring timely completion.
  • Communicate effectively with faculty, industry experts, speakers, and students.
  • Demonstrate leadership, teamwork, professionalism, creativity, and problem-solving skills.
  • Contribute ideas toward making departmental events more engaging and impactful.
  • Represent CS-CATALYST professionally and build a strong culture of academic & technical activities.
