5-Day Program Schedule

DAY 1 — Sept 07 OFFLINE

Kick-off & Hands-on Workshop

Inauguration ceremony at Activity Space-2, TIET. Introduction to qubits, superposition, Bloch sphere representation, and setup of quantum development environment.

DAYS 2–4 — Sept 08–10 ONLINE

Live Sessions, Demos & Hands-on Labs

Virtual interactive sessions via Zoom. IBM Qiskit circuit labs, Grover's Algorithm, Shor's Factoring algorithm, quantum error correction, and real platform demos.

DAY 5 — Sept 11 OFFLINE

Project Showcase, Wrap-up & Certification

Participant quantum algorithm projects presented before a panel, valedictory session, feedback collection, and e-certificate distribution ceremony.
