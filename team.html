Meet the CS-CATALYST Team

A passionate student organizing panel working under collective faculty guidance to drive technical excellence in the CSE Department, TIET.

Managed by Computer Science Faculty

CS-CATALYST is the official departmental society of Computer Science & Engineering at Thapar Institute of Engineering & Technology. The society is managed, directed, and mentored under the collective guidance of the faculty members of the Department of Computer Science & Engineering (CSED).

Faculty mentor for student coordinators: Prof. Sandeep Verma
