CS-CATALYST is under the distinguished guidance of the Computer Science & Engineering Department leadership at TIET, Patiala.

HEAD OF DEPARTMENT

Mr. Neeraj Kumar

Department of Computer Science & Engineering
Thapar Institute of Engineering & Technology (TIET), Patiala

Mr. Neeraj Kumar serves as the Head of the Department (HOD) of Computer Science & Engineering at Thapar Institute of Engineering & Technology, Patiala. Under his visionary leadership, the CSE Department has consistently grown in academic excellence, research output, and industry collaboration. He champions the CS-CATALYST Society as a cornerstone initiative for student development and departmental outreach.

His leadership has been instrumental in establishing national-level FDPs, flagship quantum computing workshops, and strengthening the department's ties with industry leaders and academic institutions across the country.

FACULTY COORDINATOR · CS-CATALYST

Dr. Sandeep Verma

Assistant Professor — Department of Computer Science & Engineering
Thapar Institute of Engineering & Technology (TIET), Patiala

Dr. Sandeep Verma is an Assistant Professor in the CSE Department at TIET, Patiala, and serves as the primary Faculty Coordinator of CS-CATALYST Society. He plays a hands-on role in designing, planning, and executing all departmental academic and technical events — from Faculty Development Programs and Short-Term Courses to expert talks, seminars, and the Q-BIT Quantum Computing Workshop series.

His research interests and teaching expertise span across emerging areas in computer science. He mentors the student organizing panel directly, guiding them on event management, professional development, documentation, and leadership skills.
